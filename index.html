Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 11, 2026

FPV Guidebook ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: When you register, we collect your email address and a display name.
  • Camera: The app requests camera access solely to allow you to take a photo for your profile avatar. Photos are not stored on our servers unless you choose to upload them.
  • Usage data: We collect anonymous product view counts to surface popular content.
  • Ratings: If you rate a product, we store your rating linked to your account.
  • Bookmarks and search history: Saved for your convenience and stored securely in our database.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your location.
  • We do not collect contacts or call logs.
  • We do not sell your data to third parties.
  • We do not display ads.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the app experience.
  • To authenticate your account securely.
  • To send in-app notifications about new product guides (can be disabled in settings).

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, hosted on servers in the EU. We use industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest.

Third-Party Services

  • Supabase – database and authentication (privacy policy)
  • Anthropic Claude API – powers the AI product assistant (privacy policy)

Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

Children's Privacy

FPV Guidebook is intended for users 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
